06 — Brand Voice & Usage
Language & Positioning
VPP is an authority, not a vendor. The language should reflect a program that shops are proud to be part of — not a tool they bought.

Brand Voice Attributes

Authoritative
We set the standard. Shops certify to meet it. Consumers trust the program.
Approachable
Expert without being intimidating. We educate, not lecture. We consult, not sell.
Data-Driven
Paint gauges, protection scores, driver profiles. Every recommendation is backed by data.
Premium
This is an investment, not an expense. We preserve value, not just apply products.

✓ Do Say

  • "Your Vehicle Preservation Center"
  • "VPP-certified advisor"
  • "Part of the Vehicle Preservation Program network"
  • "Consultation" (not "sales pitch")
  • "Protection plan" (not "package deal")
  • "Driver profile" (not "customer type")
  • "Investment" (not "cost" or "price")
  • "Preserve" and "protect" (active, positive)

✗ Don't Say

  • "Our software" or "the VPP app"
  • "White-label" or "your branded version"
  • "Powered by VPP" (VPP IS the power)
  • "Sales tool" or "closing tool"
  • "Package deal" or "bundle discount"
  • "Upsell" or "add-on revenue"
  • Shop name before VPP name
  • "Sign up for VPP" (shops "become certified")

Naming Hierarchy

The Program
Vehicle Preservation Program
The overarching brand. The authority. What consumers trust and shops certify under.
The Location
Vehicle Preservation Center
What each certified shop becomes. "Vive Auto Salon, a Vehicle Preservation Center."
The Person
VPP-Certified Advisor
The individual who's been trained and certified to deliver consultations through the program.
